RoboAI 10-Project Kit: Hardware Components

Based on the official projects outlined in the RoboAI Student Manual, here is the complete list of hardware components required to build all 10 edge AI and robotics projects.

🧠 Brain & Vision

These components are the core of the AI processing and vision capabilities:

  • ESP32-CAM Module: The main microcontroller, handling logic, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, and camera input for Vision Classification (Project 02, 05, 08).
  • USB to Serial Converter (FTDI) & Cable: Required for programming the ESP32-CAM from your computer.

🏃‍♂️ Movement & Robot Chassis

These parts allow your AI to interact with the physical world and move:

  • 1x 2-Wheel Drive (2WD) Robot Chassis Kit (includes acrylic base, caster wheel, and hardware)
  • 2x DC Gear Motors (for driving the robot in Projects 02, 03, 05, 07, 08, 10)
  • 2x Rubber Wheels
  • 1x Motor Driver Board (L298N or MX1508): To control the speed and direction of the DC motors based on the ESP32 logic.

👁️ Sensors & Edge Intelligence

These sensors gather data for the AI models and standard logic:

  • 1x IMU Sensor (MPU6050): A 6-axis accelerometer and gyroscope for gesture recognition and vibration/terrain mapping (Projects 01, 06).
  • 2x IR Line Tracking Sensors: For detecting light/dark surfaces in the Neural Line Follower (Project 03).
  • 1x Microphone Module (INMP441 or MAX9814): For capturing audio to trigger local Wake-Word detection (Project 04).
  • 1x Ultrasonic Distance Sensor (HC-SR04): For obstacle avoidance and drop-off/edge detection (Projects 08, 10).
  • 1x Environmental Sensor Bundle (DHT11/BME280 & Soil Moisture/Rain Sensor): To collect temperature, humidity, and weather data for the IoT Weather Scout (Project 09).

🔋 Power & Wiring

  • 1x Battery Pack (2x 18650 Battery Holder or 4x AA): To provide sufficient power to the ESP32 and Motors.
  • 1x Mini Breadboard: For prototyping circuits without soldering.
  • Assorted Jumper Wires (M-M, M-F, F-F): To connect all the sensors to the ESP32-CAM and Motor Driver.

🗺️ Component-to-Project Mapping

Here is how these components are used across your 10 Platform Projects:

  1. 01 Gesture Pilot: MPU6050 (IMU)
  2. 02 Vision-Based Follower: ESP32-CAM, Motors
  3. 03 Neural Line Follower: IR Sensors, Motors
  4. 04 Voice Command Bot: Microphone Module
  5. 05 Smart Traffic Car: ESP32-CAM, Motors
  6. 06 Pothole/Terrain Mapper: MPU6050 (IMU)
  7. 07 Autonomous Maze Solver: Motors (and optional Ultrasonic)
  8. 08 Obstacle Intelligence: ESP32-CAM, Ultrasonic Sensor, Motors
  9. 09 IoT Weather Scout: Environmental Sensors
  10. 10 Safety/Edge Guard: Ultrasonic Sensor, Motors
